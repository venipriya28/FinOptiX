# FinOps Foundation Framework

## Source
FinOps Framework: https://www.finops.org/framework/

## Retrieved guidance
FinOps is a collaborative operating practice that aims to maximize technology business value through timely, data-informed decisions and shared financial accountability. Workload optimization is one capability within the broader practice.

## FinOptiX interpretation
FinOptiX supports review with cost, usage, risk, and evidence summaries. It is a decision-support prototype, not a complete FinOps operating model.
